ORDER RE: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE: If any attorney for a party or a pro se party has used any generative artificial intelligence (“AI”) in the preparation of any complaint, answer, motion, brief, or other document filed with the Court, the attorney or pro se party shall disclose that AI has been used in the preparation of the document, and CERTIFY that the attorney or pro se party has personally reviewed and verified the accuracy of all legal citations, quotations, factual statements, and analyses contained in the document. Failure to include such certification will result in the striking of the document.
HEARINGS: All civil and criminal matters will be held in person unless instructed otherwise. At this time the Court is not conducting any telephonic or video conference hearings. Please do not email the clerk requesting a remote appearance. Should the parties still wish to request a remote appearance, no later than three (3) court days before the court proceeding an ex parte application and proposed order or stipulation and proposed order shall be filed. Such a request must be supported by a declaration setting forth good cause for the remote appearance. In the unlikely event the court grants the request, the type of remote appearance allowed will be at the court’s discretion.
RECORDING IS STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Recording, copying, photographing and rebroadcasting of court proceedings is prohibited by federal law.
MEDIA REQUESTS: Neither Judge Walter nor his CRD will respond to requests from the media. Media should refer to the Central District website for information and instructions on how to communicate with the Court.
