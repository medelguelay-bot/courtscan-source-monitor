IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
FOR THE EASTERN DISTRICT OF PENNSYLVANIA
STANDING ORDER RE: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN
CASES ASSIGNED TO JUDGE SCOTT
If any attorney for a party, or a pro se party, has used generative Artificial Intelligence
(“AI”)—including but not limited to ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or any other program that uses
machine learning to create new content—in a citation of any legal authority filed with the Court,
and assigned to Judge Kai N. Scott, then they MUST, in a clear and plain factual statement,
disclose that generative AI has been used to assist with the citation of legal authority, disclose
what specific generative AI program was used, and CERTIFY that each and every citation of
legal authority has been verified as accurate.
DATED: 03/03/2025 BY THE COURT:
HON. KAI N. SCOTT,
United States District Court Judge
